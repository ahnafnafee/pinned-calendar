# Pinned Calendar launch film: treatment

A 36-second launch film for the Play listing (as a YouTube link) and for social. 1920x1080,
60 fps, no voice. The look is the store screenshots' own: the app's light theme (cream
`#fff8f5`, peach `#ffdcc5`, brown `#8a5021`), the scalloped "cookie" shape from the accent
swatch, two-tone headlines (ink line, orange line) and real app UI. It is not one of the
Lemo-Opuscar library styles. The library's tools and directing method are used, as in the
Player 2 launch film this one is modeled on.

## Premise and bar

One day, start to finish, on one pinned card. The agenda sits in the notification shade
from the first swipe; the day runs across it; at midnight the card turns its own page to
tomorrow. The pushpin is the motif: it pins the card in the first seconds, holds it while
the day runs, serves as the hinge when midnight turns the page, and becomes the app icon at
the end.

The bar is the Player 2 film: every cut on the beat, no default fades, real app footage as
proof, words that land on the grid.

## Three structures, and the choice

1. **A feature list on the beat.** Five phrases, one feature each, then the logo. Clear, but
   it is every launch video, and it has no turn.
2. **Before and after.** A cluttered phone where you keep opening the calendar app, then the
   pin, then calm. The "before" spends half the film on a problem nobody wants to watch.
3. **A day on the pin (chosen).** A clock runs from 9:30 AM to midnight over one card. Every
   feature happens at the time of day it would in life: the morning list, ticks through the
   afternoon, a reminder at the oven, a recurring chore, a change of colors, and then
   midnight, when the card turns the page by itself. The arc is built in: the day itself is
   the timeline, and midnight is the turn.

## Logline and arc

One swipe shows your day pinned; the day runs across the card, and at midnight it turns the
page to tomorrow on its own. Setup: the swipe and the pin (bars 0 to 2). Development: the
day ticks by, one feature per stretch of the clock (bars 3 to 10). Turn: 11:59, silence,
the page turns (bars 11 to 14). Ending: the pin becomes the icon (bars 15 to 17), echoing
the pin that opened the film.

## Benchmark

Learn from product launch films that keep one object on a clean stage and let the interface
do the acting: one line of copy per idea, motion that lands on a grid, a single prop that
carries the whole film. Learn from desk-calendar and flip-clock time lapses: time made
visible by pages and digits turning. Do not take: anyone's interface, type, melody, logo or
shots.

## Beat sheet (120 BPM, one bar = 2.0 s)

- 0.0 to 1.0: the phone, home screen. A clock tick on each beat, nothing else.
- 1.0: the swipe. The real shade drops with the pinned agenda in it.
- 2.0: the first downbeat. A pushpin drops onto the card's top edge; the drums start.
- 2.0 to 6.0: the card lifts out and settles on the right; "Your week," / "pinned." lands on
  the left, word by word; the clock label reads 9:30 AM.
- 6.0 to 10.0: the clock runs to 6:00 PM. Rows are checked off on beats; "Reply to that 2019
  email" is swiped to tomorrow instead (the gag). "Ticks off" / "your day."
- 10.0 to 14.0: 6:58 PM, the oven reminder drops on a ding; the tap on Mark done at 12.0.
  "Mark it done." / "Or snooze it."
- 14.0 to 18.0: the repeat chips; the pill hops on eighth notes and lands on Daily; the
  chore copies across seven days. "Set it once." / "It repeats."
- 18.0 to 22.0: six accent colors, one per beat; the stage flips dark on beat 3 of bar 10
  and back. "Make it" / "yours."
- 22.0 to 25.5: 11:58, 11:59. "Midnight?" The ticks slow down.
- 25.5 to 26.0: silence.
- 26.0: the page turns over the pin; TODAY now reads THU 26. "Already tomorrow." lands.
- 26.0 to 30.0: the new day's rows settle; a breath.
- 30.0 to 36.0: the pin lifts off the card and lands in the app icon; "Pinned Calendar", then
  "Free on Google Play", then "No account. No ads. Open source."

## Camera and transitions

One world-to-screen function serves every element. Transitions happen inside the picture
and share one grammar, the pin: the pin lifts, the old object leaves, and the pin lands on
the next one on the downbeat. Text leaves by folding flat onto its baseline. The signature
shot is the page turn at midnight: the card rotates over the top edge with the pin as the
hinge, and the new day is underneath.

## Sound design

| Section | Bed | Foley | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| open | a quiet room tone | ticks, the swipe, the pin's wooden thump | muffled pluck, then the drop |
| day | room tone | a pencil tick per check, a swipe for the email | groove |
| nudge | none | the notification ding, a tap, a pop as the card folds | groove |
| repeat | none | chip clicks, seven soft pops | groove with sixteenths |
| yours | none | a pop per color, a switch click for dark | groove |
| midnight | room tone | ticks that slow, then nothing; a paper turn and a thump on the turn | drums out, a held chord, silence, F on the turn |
| logo | none | the pin's thump, a soft chime | stab, plucks tail off |

Two silences: the opening bar (ticks and room tone only) and the half beat before the turn.
Two sound transitions: the reminder ding arrives a beat before its scene (J-cut), and the
midnight ticks continue under the end of the colors scene (L-cut).

## Type

Google Sans Flex (the app's own face, rounded axis at 100): 800 for headlines, line 1 in ink
`#221a15`, line 2 in orange `#c75f14`; 600 for the clock label above the headline, with a
small orange dot. Words land one per half beat. App UI inside the cards uses the same face
at the app's sizes.

No voice and no subtitles: the film reads on mute, so every idea is a short on-screen line.
