# Pinned Calendar: To-Do Agenda

## Short description

Your week’s calendar and to-dos in one always-on pin. Private and offline.

## Full description

Keep your week visible without opening another app. Pinned Calendar puts upcoming events and built-in to-dos in one ongoing notification, so the next thing on your agenda is always one glance away.

📌 One always-on agenda
Your agenda stays in the notification shade and restores itself after an accidental swipe, a reboot, or an app update. Choose Top, Normal, or Silent priority, or enable swipe-twice-to-remove when you want the pin to be easier to dismiss.

🗓️ Your calendars, together
Pinned Calendar reads calendars already synced to your Android device and color-codes their events. Choose the calendars to show and look ahead 3 days, through the end of the week, 7 days, or 14 days.

✅ To-dos and actionable reminders
Add tasks with due dates, times, notes, and priorities. Repeat them daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly, or set a custom interval, weekdays, end date, or occurrence count. Unfinished tasks carry forward, while completing a recurring task advances it to its next date.

To-do reminders are enabled by default. When one appears, mark the task Done directly from the notification or Snooze it for 30 minutes without changing its original due time.

🎨 Make it yours
Use Material You dynamic color, selectable seed colors and palette styles, AMOLED black, custom fonts, compact or comfortable layouts, and light, dark, or system themes.

🔒 Private, offline, and simple
Pinned Calendar has no INTERNET permission. It never uploads your calendar or tasks, and it does not require a Google sign-in. There are no accounts, ads, trackers, or analytics. Calendar data and to-dos stay on your device.

The app only requests the permissions needed to read your device calendars, show notifications, restore the pin after startup, and optionally improve background reliability on devices with aggressive battery management.
