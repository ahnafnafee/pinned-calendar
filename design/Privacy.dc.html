Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: JUNE 23, 2026
IN SHORT

Pinned Calendar does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no servers. Everything the app uses stays on your device.

01

Information we collect

We do not collect any information from you. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to any third party, because the app has no backend and makes no network requests for your data.

To do its job, the app works with the following information locally, on your device only:

  • Calendar events. Read access to the calendars you choose, used to build this week's agenda inside a notification.
  • Your to-dos. Tasks you create are stored in the app's local storage on your device.
  • Your settings. Preferences such as theme, accent color, time window, and priority are saved locally.
02

How information is used

The information above is used for one purpose only: to display your upcoming events and to-dos as an on-device notification and inside the app. It is never used for advertising, profiling, or analytics, and it never leaves your device.

03

Permissions we request

  • Calendar (read). So the app can show your events. It never edits or deletes your calendar.
  • Notifications. So the app can post the pinned agenda to your notification shade.
  • Run at startup. So the pinned notification can rebuild itself after your phone restarts.
04

Sharing and third parties

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data, because we never receive it. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, trackers, or crash reporters that send your data off the device.

05

Retention and deletion

Your to-dos and settings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes all locally stored data permanently.

06

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, regardless of age.

07

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app's listing.

08

Contact
