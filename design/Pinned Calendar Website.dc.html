WHAT MAKES IT STICK

A calendar that meets you where you already look.

01

Always-on agenda

A persistent, self-healing notification keeps this week's plan at the top of your shade, grouped by day. It rebuilds itself after reboots, so it is always current.

02

Calendar and to-dos

Your real events and quick local to-dos sit side by side. Add a task due today in a single tap, check it off, and watch it leave the pin.

03

Private by default

No account, no analytics, no servers. The app reads your calendar on-device to build the notification and sends nothing, anywhere.

04

Made yours

Material You theming, six accent colors, light or dark, AMOLED black, and a time window you control, from three days to two weeks.

A LOOK INSIDE

Clean, fast, and right where you need it.

DATA HANDLING

Your data never leaves your phone.

No sign-in. No analytics. No servers. Pinned Calendar builds the notification locally and keeps your to-dos and settings on your device only.
